Georgia Knife Rules — The Short Version

Four things worth knowing before you carry

Georgia at a glance Updated 1 September 2026
Blades 12″ and under
Carry freelyGeorgia defines a “knife” as a blade greater than 12 inches. Anything at or under that is not a weapon under state law and needs no licence.
Blades over 12″
Licence neededCounts as a weapon under O.C.G.A. 16-11-125.1. Carrying one beyond your home, property, vehicle or business needs a Georgia weapons carry licence.
Switchblades & automatics
LegalGeorgia law contains no switchblade ban. The 12-inch test is the only one that matters.
City rules
PreemptedO.C.G.A. 16-11-173 bars counties and cities from regulating weapons, and the definition expressly includes bladed devices.

Sources: O.C.G.A. 16-11-125.1 — Definitions · O.C.G.A. 16-11-173 — Preemption · Georgia Knife Laws. General information only — Michigan Sports Outdoor is a retailer, not a law firm. Check your city ordinance before you carry.

The 12-inch line surprises people. Georgia raised it from five inches in 2017, so almost every folder and hunting knife sold today sits well inside “carry freely” territory. Everything on this page is under 3.6″.
