## Notes

- **California and Florida lead on wild pig, not deer.** Both deer seasons are
  heavily zone-split and largely finished by early November, which makes a poor
  year-round promo. Wild pig is open every day of the year with no bag limit in
  California, and year-round on private land in Florida.
- **Zone splits are stated, not averaged.** New York runs Northern and Southern
  separately, Texas North and South, North Carolina opens 17 Oct in the east and
  28 Nov in the mountains, Pennsylvania opens early in WMUs 2B, 5C and 5D.
- **Re-check every August.** These are set annually and most states publish the
  following year's dates in spring. Update `season_sub` in `build_states.py` and
  rerun — one edit per state, all pages regenerate together.
