New York Knife Rules — The Short Version

Four things worth knowing before you carry

New York at a glance Updated 1 September 2026
Gravity knives
Legal since 2019Repealed from the Penal Law weapon lists on 30 May 2019. Simple possession is no longer a crime.
Switchblades
ProhibitedStill listed in Penal Law 265.01(1), with only narrow exceptions such as the hunting and fishing exception in 265.20(6).
Folding knives
LegalOrdinary folders are legal to own and carry. Keep them fully out of sight in public.
New York City
StricterNYC requires any knife to be completely concealed in public — a visible pocket clip is enough to breach it.

Sources: Penal Law 265.01 — Criminal possession of a weapon · New York Knife Laws. General information only — Michigan Sports Outdoor is a retailer, not a law firm. Check your city ordinance before you carry.

We do not ship automatic or switchblade knives to New York. Switchblades remain prohibited under Penal Law 265.01(1), so they are filtered out of every New York collection. If you carry in New York City, keep the clip out of sight — a visible knife is a breach there even when the blade is legal.
