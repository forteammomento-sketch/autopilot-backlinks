Illinois Knife Rules — The Short Version

Four things worth knowing before you carry

Illinois at a glance Updated 1 September 2026
Folding knives
LegalConventional folding knives are legal to own and carry in Illinois with no permit.
Switchblades & OTF
FOID card only720 ILCS 5/24-1(a)(1) prohibits switchblades, but 24-1(e)(2) exempts a person holding a currently valid FOID card, which requires being 21 or over.
Ballistic knives
Banned outrightProhibited in Illinois with no exemption at all.
City rules
They applyLocal ordinances often run stricter than state law — Chicago in particular. A three-inch threshold also appears in 720 ILCS 5/33A-1(c)(2), mainly for publicly funded land and buildings.

Sources: 720 ILCS 5/24-1 — Unlawful use of weapons · Illinois Knife Laws. General information only — Michigan Sports Outdoor is a retailer, not a law firm. Check your city ordinance before you carry.

We do not ship automatic or OTF knives to Illinois. They are lawful only for holders of a valid Illinois FOID card, and we have no way to verify that at checkout. Every knife on this page is a conventional folder that needs no card. If you carry in Chicago, check the city ordinance as well — Illinois has no knife preemption.
