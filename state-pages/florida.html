Do you need a licence to carry a concealed knife in Florida?

Not since 1 July 2023. House Bill 543 extended permitless concealed carry to weapons including knives, so an adult 21 or over who meets the criteria for a Concealed Weapon Licence may carry concealed without holding one — carrying valid ID.

Source: HB 543 staff analysis

What size knife can you carry in Florida?

Florida statute sets no blade-length limit. The four-inch figure people quote comes from case law defining a “common pocketknife” under 790.001 — the Florida Supreme Court treated 4″ or less as presumptively common, and found a 3.75″ blade fell inside the exception.

Source: Fla. Stat. 790.001

Are switchblades legal in Florida?

Yes under state law — Florida has no switchblade ban. Local rules can still differ: Miami-Dade County maintains its own switchblade ordinance, so check the county you are carrying in.

Source: AKTI — Florida

Are ballistic knives legal in Florida?

No — ballistic knives are banned outright. Fla. Stat. 790.225 makes it unlawful to manufacture, display, sell, own, possess or use a ballistic self-propelled knife, meaning one that fires its blade as a projectile.

Source: Fla. Stat. 790.225

Can you open carry a knife in Florida?

Generally yes. Fla. Stat. 790.053, which restricts open carry, is written around firearms and electric weapons — it does not cover knives.

Source: Fla. Stat. 790.053

Can Florida cities make their own knife rules?

Yes — Florida has no knife preemption. Fla. Stat. 790.33 preempts firearms and ammunition only, so counties and cities may add knife restrictions of their own.

Source: Fla. Stat. 790.33
