Pennsylvania Knife Rules — The Short Version

Four things worth knowing before you carry

Pennsylvania at a glance Updated 1 September 2026
Automatics & balisongs
LegalAct 119 of 2022 removed automatic knives from the prohibited offensive weapons definition in 18 Pa.C.S. 908, effective 2 January 2023.
Blade length
No limitPennsylvania sets no statewide blade-length restriction for ordinary carry.
Philadelphia
Ban struck downA federal court declared the city’s cutting-weapons carry policy unenforceable in 2023 and enjoined Philadelphia from enforcing it.
Intent and location
Still applyThe repeal covered the weapon category only. Carrying with unlawful intent, and restricted places, remain offences — a first-degree misdemeanour carries up to 5 years and $10,000.

Sources: Act 119 of 2022 — amending 18 Pa.C.S. 908 · Pennsylvania Knife Laws. General information only — Michigan Sports Outdoor is a retailer, not a law firm. Check your city ordinance before you carry.
